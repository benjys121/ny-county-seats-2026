Are more seats being contested?

In the 15 county legislatures that elect every member in both 2025 and 2026, contested seats rose from 112 of 257 (44%) to 141 (55%). Across all county legislatures, 54% of seats on this year's ballot are contested, up from 48% in 2025 and 42% in 2023, though each year covers a different mix of counties.
